# LinkedIn: I Revised Three Novels Last Week

I revised three novels last week. Not "asked AI to rewrite them." Revised.

I'm legally blind and neurodivergent. Revision — the slow, visual, repetitive editorial grind — was the thing that kept ten manuscripts sitting in folders for years. So I built the thing that was missing.

A multi-agent editorial pipeline. Specialized agents: structural editor, line editor, continuity checker, sensitivity reader, editor-in-chief. They don't write. They read, flag, and suggest. I decide.

Last week: *The Two-Flat Cats* (queer speculative, 70K words). *Bait and Switch* (revised YA allegory). *Single Player Co-Op* (memoir about DID, blindness, survival). Three manuscripts through the pipeline.

The structural editor caught redundant setup between chapters. The continuity editor found a timeline error I'd missed for three years. The line editor tightened sentences the way I would have, given six more hours.

This isn't replacement. It's infrastructure. The same way a recording studio is infrastructure for a musician.

Every story starts in my head. Every final word is my decision. I just built the editing floor that makes finishing possible.
